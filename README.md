### Publish and verify

1. Upload the complete project, including `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `assets/`, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`.
2. Confirm these URLs open publicly in a browser:
	- `https://apnabeautandsilayicenter.in/`
	- `https://apnabeautandsilayicenter.in/robots.txt`
	- `https://apnabeautandsilayicenter.in/sitemap.xml`
3. Confirm the HTTPS certificate works and the preferred URL redirects consistently to HTTPS.
4. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and add the domain property. DNS verification is recommended; URL-prefix verification is also available.
5. In Search Console, open **Sitemaps**, enter `sitemap.xml`, and click **Submit**.
6. Open **URL Inspection**, enter the homepage URL, click **Test Live URL**, then use **Request Indexing** if the live test succeeds.
7. After a few days, review **Pages**, **Sitemaps**, **Core Web Vitals** and **Enhancements** in Search Console. Fix any reported crawl or mobile usability errors.
8. Create or claim the Google Business Profile using the exact real business name, phone number, address and category. Keep those details consistent with the website.
9. Add the real Google Business Profile, Instagram and Facebook URLs only after they exist. Replace the placeholder social values in `script.js`.
10. Submit the same sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools for additional discovery.
